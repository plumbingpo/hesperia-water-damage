The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
